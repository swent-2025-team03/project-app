## Completeness

We first evaluated the depth and complexity of the main epics in your app, along with their contribution to the app, the tangible value they provide to the user, and their alignment with the app’s goals.

We evaluated the extent to which your app meets the course requirements articulated at the start of the semester, and whether they are implemented effectively, they integrate seamlessly, and are indeed essential to the app.

We also looked at the robustness and completeness of the different features you implemented: are all the features finished and polished, are they secure and bug-free, and are they thoughtfully designed.

In general not much to say, you cover more than 2 epics, you meet the requirements of the course and beyond. Your features are meaningful and mostly complete and polished. Just two remarks about the completeness of features:

- On the account page there is a field for email that just displays "Email address". It may be a good idea to display the real email address.

- Also on the same screen, while being a vet, I have the category "Share anonymous" data, but I can never edit it (because it's a feature reserved for farmer right?). It may be good to let vets edit it or to remove it from vets profiles.

## Functionality

In this context, we assessed your app's ability to handle unexpected inputs provided by clueless or malicious users (including spamming buttons, entering wrong inputs, stopping a process mid-way, etc.); we wanted to see that your app handles all edge cases gracefully, has comprehensive error handling, and includes robust mechanisms for maintaining stability under stress.

We then evaluated the performance and reliability of the final product, i.e., the APK: we wanted to see that your APK is stable and the UI responds quickly and has seamless navigation.

Next we looked into your implementation of user authentication and multi-user support: does the app correctly manage users, can users personalize their accounts, does the app support session persistence, are multi-user interactions well supported, can accounts be used on another device, and is account information preserved when switching devices.

The core flows are solid, it supports everything essential that your users would need. You implemented an original idea with technical features and the functionality was based on user needs, this is great!

One issue that could arise with clueless users: when singning up as a vet, I was able to go back with the "go back" action and chose farmer to re-signup with the same account. I don't know how this reflected on the database.


For this part, you received 5.6/6.

## User Experience

For this part, we wanted to see how intuitive and user-friendly the app is for real users. Beyond having good usability, did you pay attention to streamlining the interactions, is it easy to figure out, can new users start making good use of the app quickly, are the interaction flows well thought out and refined.

There is no onboarding or guidance for new users, we would say it's ok because the app is quite intuitive to use, but note that it could be used by people that aren't used to technology.

The map feature with location, reports and alerts is really cool, maybe you could have shown it during the BOAT. In general good, two small issues with the information provided to users:

- When signing up as a vet it "asks" for a Clinic address, but we can't enter any neither change the location. Then only after signing up, we can go onto "Manage My Offices", create an office, then go back to the profile, edit the profile and then I can input a location. The functionality is fine but it's not very intuitive.

- Also when sining up with google, for some reason it gives me a password (that I don't know), and I should be able to change it if I knew it, this is a bit confusing. It also shows on the profile page.

- When creating a report without filling in all fields, it says “Please fill in all required fields…”, but nothing points out which fields are required and which are not. So we would suggest either adding a red note on the required fields or say “Please fill in all fields…” for clarity

- Again on the clarity of errors, when trying to generate a code I got “Something went wrong, please make sure you are connected to the internet.” but actually it was because I left my office and had no office. It took me a little time to understand because the error wasn’t clear.


For this part, you received 5.3/6.

## Design and Maintainability

We evaluated whether your code is of high quality and employs best practices.
We expect the codebase to be polished, well documented, follow consistent conventions, be modular, and allow for easy modifications.
You should be able to employ advanced techniques by now, such as asynchronous functions (flows, coroutines), good resource management, and automated dependency injection (e.g., with Hilt).

We also assessed your overall app architecture and design, looking in particular at aspects surrounding robustness and scalability.
We looked at both the codebase and the documentation of the app (Wiki and architecture diagram), we expect your design to demonstrate thoughtful consideration for performance, maintainability, and future growth.

In general the code is clean, it uses the right patterns and is readable.

But still, some PRs merged code that clearly lacked modularity and was unnecessarily verbose. Sonar still points out some really high function complexity on a few functions on main, this is a sign of a lack of modularity.

And also here are still a substancial number of Sonar issues with the code on main. Even if we agree that individually they aren't much of an issue, becasue it's often minor maintainability issues, this is still an accumulation of technical debt.


For this part, you received 5.1/6.

## QA (Testing)

The first aspect we looked at here was your test suite, in terms of both quality and the final line coverage.
We expect the testing to be rigorous and to cover all components and edge cases, and they should validate every significant user journey.
Your end-to-end tests should be detailed and include error-handling scenarios.
The tests should be well-documented and easy to maintain.
Finally, your test suite should demonstrate advanced techniques, mock data for performance testing, and automated regression tests.

In general, you focus more on the quality of the code than on the quality of the tests. The test quality is a bit inconsistent from times to times. We think that you can improve on the quality and robustness of tests by automating some "typical use actions" and modulaising them. This is pretty much about following the same patterns for the tests as for normal code.


For this part, you received 5.45/6.

## Documentation

We looked at your README and GitHub Wiki to evaluate the quality and completeness of your app’s documentation. We expect the README and Wiki to be thorough and achieve professional-level clarity and completeness.
They should provide detailed descriptions of the app's architecture, implementation of the features, the development setup and guidelines for contributing.

We also assessed your use of Figma and the architecture diagram for effective UI design, organization, and app structure planning.
By this stage, we expect your Figma and Architecture diagram to be complete and up-to-date. The Figma should be consistent with the UI and the Architecture diagram should be comprehensive.

The main README is good, but for a finished project, we expect a bit more. It would be good to add a description of the tech stack, the installation prerequisites and a tutorial to setup the project for potential contributors.

The wiki include the old architecture diagram in the section "Architecture", it would maybe have been good to include versions and to indicate that it isn't the last version. In general, the architecture diagram is clear and up to date. There are just the interactions with the codes to link farmers and vers and also vets and offices that aren't really clear on the diagram, it could include a bit more details on that.  Also we think it could be good to include more details on the data layer about the attributes of the various models. Figma is up to date and the UI is great.

Also, there is developer-oriented docs in the wiki section named "Team process". It would be good to make it more general for any contributor.

Finally, in general the inline comments in the code are good, but they are sometimes a bit too general. They don't always provide good insight in how some functions work. The Kdoc is good, but sometimes additional explanation of structure and implementation would be good.



For this part, you received 5.1/6.
